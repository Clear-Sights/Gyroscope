  Use before any call that decides what the rest of the run inherits — dispatching work, pushing,
  deleting, finalizing a plan, adopting a report as fact, accepting an optimization claim, choosing
  how much to spend, or writing a rule with a default. At those moments one cheap call prevents a
  loss out of all proportion to it: probe the capability before spending it, fetch the artifact
  instead of trusting the claim, check the branch before committing, freeze the baseline before
  testing an improvement, run the smallest candidate first, run the check with its input removed.
  Also use when a behaviour keeps recurring despite
  instructions — overclaiming, skipping verification, treating missing input as success, declaring
  done without evidence — to reverse the effort so the wrong move costs a machine-checkable artifact
  and the right move is free. Ships a checker: it reports PASS, FAIL or NOT_EVALUABLE over a
  supplied reversal relation, and establishes structure only, never outcome efficacy.

## OC-SMALLEST — smallest sufficient amount

**Do:** Before spending work, calls, agents, scope, or authority, freeze the bar the result must
clear and name its unit. Enumerate the candidate amounts in ascending order, run the smallest
candidate first, and take the first candidate that clears the bar on an actual run. Spend exactly
that amount and record the amount spent, its unit, the bar, and every smaller candidate that failed.
Re-derive the amount whenever the bar moves.

**You hold:** The smallest sufficient amount established by observed runs, with the decision and
its evidence kept together. This clause can finish with less action; completing its procedure does
not automatically add another requirement to the governed work.

## DW-MEASURE — baseline before optimization

**Do:** Before running a candidate whose claimed gain will justify a decision or effect, freeze a
baseline comparison contract. Bind the subject and claimed effect; measure definition; population
or workload; measurement method; window, conditions, and exclusions; baseline source, value,
as-of time, and digest; uncertainty or error bound; target or acceptable range; and decision
threshold and rule. After the run, measure the candidate on that same basis and emit a comparison
receipt binding the contract to the after-state result, delta, and threshold verdict. Refuse the
improvement claim and every effect that depends on it when the contract or receipt is missing, the
baseline was derived from the candidate result, or the measure, population, method, window,
conditions, exclusions, or threshold changed. A changed basis starts a new baseline.

**You hold:** A pre-candidate baseline and a same-basis comparison receipt. They establish that the
claimed comparison is admissible; they do not establish that the chosen measure is wise, that the
candidate caused the result, or that outcome efficacy follows.

## AP-DUAL-ORIGIN — independent admission receipt

**Do:** Before accepting a performer's report as evidence for its own consequential effect, freeze
the exact subject, claim, effect, acceptance criteria, and required observations. Obtain those
observations by a source the performer cannot populate — direct inspection, automatic capture, or
independent reperformance — and have an authorized verifier derive a bounded machine-readable
receipt. Bind the receipt to the subject and effect digests, evidence-source identities and digests,
criteria, performer and verifier identities and authority, observation time, and verdict. The
accepting transition itself verifies and consumes the receipt. It refuses an absent or self-issued
receipt, a nominally different verifier backed by the performer's credential or mutable evidence,
and a receipt for another subject, claim, or effect.

**You hold:** An effect-bound independent admission receipt whose evidence origin can be checked
without relying on the report it admits. It establishes the receipt's provenance and the declared
check, not outcome efficacy; a second signature over the performer's account is still self-report.

**Field corroboration:** Audit and assurance supplies direct confirmation or independent
reperformance ([`prior-art/audit.md`](prior-art/audit.md)); metrology supplies a result-specific
record with a traceable evidence chain ([`prior-art/metrology.md`](prior-art/metrology.md)); aviation
supplies independently captured operational traces ([`prior-art/aviation.md`](prior-art/aviation.md));

## CI-VALIDITY — use-time validity witness

**Do:** Before an act relies on a reading to accept a report, delete, or push, bind the exact
target and effect to every authoritative source and dependency. Record each source identity and
version or digest, when it was observed, its validity rule or valid-through boundary, and every
condition on which that validity depends. At the effect boundary, re-resolve those sources and
dependencies and evaluate the current time and conditions. Mint the witness only while all fields
still match and remain valid; the same transition must verify and consume that one-use witness.
Missing evidence, expiry, a changed source or dependency, an unmet condition, or a wrong target or
effect refuses the act even when the originally read bytes are unchanged.

**You hold:** A machine-readable, effect-bound witness of validity at use, not a remembered reading
or a promise to refresh it later. The witness establishes only the declared reading's admission at
that boundary; it does not establish outcome efficacy.
